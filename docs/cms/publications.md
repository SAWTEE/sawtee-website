# Publications & research

## Publications

Use for volumes and downloadable reports tied to the publications taxonomy.

Typical fields:

- Title, subtitle, volume / volume slug  
- Category (under publications)  
- Description  
- Featured image  
- PDF file (moved into the publications storage path)

Public links often look like:

`/category/publications/{category}/{volume-slug}`  
or a direct publications file URL when no volume slug is set.

Tag a publication **`featured`** to appear in the home “Featured publications” rail (when that homepage section is enabled).

## Research

Research entries are similar (title, year, description, image, file, SEO fields) but use the research archive templates and `Research_Reports` storage path. Keep years accurate — the public research list is ordered by year.
