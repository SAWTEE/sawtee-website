# Teams & fellowships

## Team

Staff profiles: name, email, designation, bio, order, photo. Ordered lists appear on team archive pages.

## Fellowships & fellows

- **Fellowship** — programme year/title/description  
- **Fellow** — person linked to a fellowship, with photo and bio  
- **Published stories** — external links / galleries tied to fellows  

The intro and empty-state wording on `/media-fellows` are in [Site Settings → Media fellows](./site-settings). Fellow names, bios, and stories are still edited here.

## Member institutes

Country **members** and related **institutes** (name, link, logo) for network pages.
