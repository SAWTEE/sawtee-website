# Logging in

1. Visit `/admin` (or `/admin/login`).  
2. Sign in with your staff account.  
3. Complete email verification if prompted.  
4. You land on the **Dashboard**.

## Tips

- Use a password manager; do not share accounts.  
- If you are locked out, ask an admin to reset your user or use “Forgot password”.  
- Staging and production have **separate** databases and users unless your team deliberately shared them (not recommended).
